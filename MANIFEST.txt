D.O.G.M.A. - release feature manifest
version=0.1.0

Lists features shipped by tools/package-fomod.sh (GitHub release zip / MO2 FOMOD).
Generated by tools/package-fomod.sh - do not hand-edit; change src/<cat>/<feat>/installer/ instead.

Always installed:
  common - shared D.O.G.M.A. core (MCM, dogma_common, banner, shared strings)

Optional features (24, FOMOD per-category SelectAny):

[crafting]
  ammo                   Ammo                   recommended
    Ammo crafting always uses one casing and one bullet per box. Overrides Ammo Maker recipes.
[fx]
  radiation              Radiation              recommended
    Radiation grain and colour effects track your actual dose. Replaces the stock radiation screen effect.
[hud]
  fps                    FPS                    optional
    On-screen FPS counter with optional graph.
  nvg                    NVG                    recommended
    Night vision remembers on or off across map changes.
[items]
  disassemble            Disassemble            recommended
    Keybinds to disassemble items from inventory.
  drop                   Drop                   recommended
    Keybinds to drop items from inventory.
  favourites             Favourites             recommended
    Keeps SortingPlus favourites between new games. Requires SortingPlus.
  stripping              Stripping              recommended
    Keybinds to field-strip weapons in inventory and loot.
[mcm]
  search                 MCM Search             recommended
    Search box for the Mod Configuration Menu.
[misc]
  missions               Missions               recommended
    Unlocks Afterglow as soon as Mortal Sin is done.
  remember_options       Remember Options       recommended
    Remembers New Game difficulty and modes.
  rf_receiver            RF Receiver            recommended
    RF packages can spawn on the map you are on. Needs Tweaki_Breeki / Cookbook RF packages.
[mutants]
  skinning               Skinning               optional
    Faster mutant skinning. Optional look-at replaces FDDA's mutant-skin camera when enabled.
  tweaks                 Tweaks                 recommended
    Jump to dodge pseudogiant ground slams.
[npcs]
  speed                  Speed                  recommended
    Slows stalker combat movement. ISG is unchanged.
[pda]
  stay_down              Stay Down              recommended
    Stops the PDA jumping back into your hands after inventory or teleports. Disable any other don't-reequip-PDA mod if you have one.
[player]
  pose_controls          Pose Controls          optional
    Crouch crouches, a walk key walks, and low-crouch goes prone.
  real_prone             Real Prone             recommended
    Makes the player actually go prone rather than just low crouch.
  scanner_keys           Scanner Keys           recommended
    Hotkeys for detector, dosimeter, and RF receiver. Guns can use the detector slot.
[tooltips]
  loadout                Loadout                recommended
    Proper item tooltips on the New Game loadout screen. Works with Advanced Item Stats and Weapons tooltips.
  weapons                Weapons                recommended
    Adds detailed weapon performance stats to weapon tooltips. Works with Advanced Item Stats.
[travel]
  fast_travel            Fast Travel            optional
    Travel from the PDA map with paths, ambushes, and sneak options.
[zoom]
  ads_zoom               ADS Zoom               recommended
    Extra zoom while aiming, by irons, sights, or scopes. Suppresses Adjustable Scope View and similar ADS FOV mods; disable those if you use them.
  free_zoom              Free Zoom              recommended
    Zoom your view without aiming. Suppresses GAMMA / Kute / SlowVibe Free Zoom; disable those if you use them.
